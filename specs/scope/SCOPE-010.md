Version: v1.1

---

# 1. System Purpose

This system exists to build a stable, low-risk, predictable recurring revenue asset by providing a deterministic inspection-interval tracking infrastructure for German KMU.

The dominant objective is economic stability.

All structural and regulatory boundaries serve the goal of:

- minimizing support burden
- avoiding interpretative complexity
- preventing integration pressure
- ensuring operational predictability

If functional expansion conflicts with simplicity, simplicity wins.

---

# 2. Authoritative Target Definition

Geographic scope: Germany only (Phase 1 frozen)

Primary qualification metric:
≥ 50 inspection-relevant assets

Secondary structural proxy:
20–150 employees

Clarification:

- A company with 10 employees and 100 assets qualifies.
- A company with 100 employees and 5 assets does not qualify.

The number of assets is the authoritative ICP filter.

---

# 3. Responsibility Doctrine

Object inclusion responsibility lies 100% with the user.

The system is a structured container.

The system does not:

- evaluate whether an object is inspection-relevant
- provide decision trees
- provide compliance assistance
- suggest object inclusion

Users decide what to track.

The system records and calculates only.

---

# 4. Interval Responsibility Doctrine

The system provides a static interval selection:

- 3 months
- 6 months
- 12 months
- 24 months

There is no system-side linkage between:

Object class ↔ Interval

Users independently choose:

- object class
- interval

Static regulatory references (e.g., DGUV V3) may be displayed
for informational context only.

No interval is implied or recommended by the system.

The system does not interpret regulations.

---

# 5. Product Nature

The system is:

- A documentation infrastructure
- A deterministic deadline calculator
- A historical record keeper
- An audit-export generator

The system is not:

- A compliance advisor
- A legal interpreter
- A risk-scoring engine
- A safety management system
- An ERP extension
- A workflow engine

---

# 6. Onboarding Doctrine

Allowed:

- Technical assistance
- CSV import guidance
- Data entry service into the fixed system schema

Not allowed:

- Advising on interval selection
- Advising on object classification
- Reviewing regulatory correctness

If the customer cannot provide interval data,
the system does not infer it.

Doctrine:

We are data typists, not safety engineers.

---

# 7. Reminder Boundary

Reminder channel: Email only.

Trigger logic:

Status transition only:

- Green → Yellow
- Yellow → Red

Recipient:

Assigned responsible role only.

No escalation logic.
No management workflows.
No hierarchical routing.

---

# 8. Revenue Doctrine

This is a lifestyle-stable asset.

It operates under strict kill-if-friction discipline.

Every 6 months a formal erosion check is executed.

Termination preparation is mandatory if:

- Average support effort exceeds 15 minutes per customer per month
- Customers demand non-deterministic features to remain
- Legal interpretation pressure becomes structural
- Integration becomes market-expected

Optionality over ego.

---

# 9. Determinism Priority

If forced to choose between:

- Regulatory exhaustiveness
- Structural simplicity

Structural simplicity wins.

---

# 10. Final Intent Statement

This system exists to provide German KMU with a simple,
deterministic inspection-tracking infrastructure that:

- replaces Excel tracking
- calculates deadlines
- records inspection history
- produces audit-ready exports

without expanding into advisory,
interpretative,
integrative,
or workflow complexity.

If this boundary erodes, the asset must not evolve —
it must be exited.
